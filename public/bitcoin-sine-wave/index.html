Two cycles is not a track record

Those 82 months are not 82 independent bets. With a 42-month wave, 2019 to 2026 holds about two cycles, so the hit rate comes from being right through roughly one rise and one fall. The wave has also been late at every turning point so far, which is tolerable at a bottom and expensive at a top.

A wave is a description, not a mechanism

Three completed cycles is a thin record to fit a period to, and the wave has placed earlier turning points several months from where they actually fell. Read the position as a rough bearing. The halving schedule, liquidity and leverage drive the price; the sine only summarises what they have done so far.

How the wave was fitted

Daily BTC-USD closes from Yahoo Finance, taken at each month end. The trend is a power law in Bitcoin's age: log price regressed on the log of days since the genesis block on 3 January 2009. One sine was fitted jointly with that trend by least squares, trying every period from 24 to 84 months in quarter-month steps and keeping the best. Position is measured from the wave's trough, so 0% is the bottom, 25% halfway up, 50% the top, 75% halfway down. The vintage chart repeats the whole procedure on each month's data alone.
